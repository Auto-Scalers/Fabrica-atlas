# Synthesis Sub-Domain: Decision Matrix

## Subroutines
1. **Multi-Factor Scoring**: Evaluate options across Benefit (HIGH/MED/LOW), Cost (HIGH/MED/LOW), and Risk (HIGH/MED/LOW).
2. **Worth-It Determination**: Compute binary or weighted recommendation (`worth_it: YES/NO`).
3. **Execution Sequencing**: Rank backlog items by high impact / low cost ratio for rapid execution.
