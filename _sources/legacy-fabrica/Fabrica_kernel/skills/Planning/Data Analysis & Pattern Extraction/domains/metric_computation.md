# Analysis Sub-Domain: Metric Computation

## Subroutines
1. **Aggregations & Ratios**: Compute sums, averages, percentiles (p50, p95, p99), and growth velocity.
2. **Financial & Unit Economics**: Calculate CAC, LTV, gross margin, burn rate, and payback periods.
3. **Cohort & Conversion Analysis**: Evaluate funnel drop-off rates and cohort retention curves.
